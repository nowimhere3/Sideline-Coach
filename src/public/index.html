Sideline will try out a few AI models from OpenRouter to see which make good Scouts. Each tryout has a model read a few project files from your open Game (such as its manifest and README), so that content is sent to that provider. Tryouts can use your provider quota or hit rate limits, may download or set up provider tooling, and can take several minutes. Nothing runs until you start it.
