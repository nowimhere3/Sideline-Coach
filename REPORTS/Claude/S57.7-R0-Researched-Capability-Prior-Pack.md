## 8. Normalization Method

S57.1 fixes the Beta form, the caps and the decay, but it does not say how qualitative research becomes m₀. These rules are the **smallest conservative conversion** that fills that gap. The generator applies them mechanically, and the validator re-checks them (§15).

| Rule | Statement |
|---|---|
| **N1 Ordinal only** | Benchmark figures only order configurations *within one source and harness*. They never set m₀'s magnitude and are never compared across harnesses. |
| **N2 Grade → m₀** | **A 0.72 · B 0.66 · C 0.60 · D 0.54.** The model grade comes from its cross-source standing. An effort step moves one grade only if the within-source gap is **≥ 5 points** or the vendor explicitly steers that work away. Smaller gaps do not change the grade. |
