## 4. WHY THE FIX IS CORRECT

1. **Provider Alignment:** The protocol schema generated by Codex app-server (defined in `v2/AccountRateLimitsUpdatedNotification.json` and `#definitions/AccountRateLimitsUpdatedNotification` in `codex_app_server_protocol.v2.schemas.json`) names the notification payload definition `AccountRateLimitsUpdatedNotification` to mirror the RPC method `account/rateLimits/updated`.
2. **Defect Origin:** During Play 5.2 (commit `298f072`), `RateLimitsUpdatedNotification` was erroneously added without the `Account` prefix. The error was masked because mock tests in `test/fixtures/fake-codex-app-server.mjs` synthesize schema definitions directly from `REQUIRED_CONTRACT.fields`, and `0.155.1` was seeded in `SEEDED_PROVEN_VERSIONS`.
3. **Exact Resolution:** Correcting the identifier to `AccountRateLimitsUpdatedNotification` aligns Sideline's requirement table with the provider's generated JSON schema. `checkSchemaContract()` now passes 100% of all required contract items against the real Codex CLI.
