==================================================
CONTINUATION AUTHORITY CONTRACT
==================================================

Automatic continuation exists only for an unconstrained AUTO decision carrying
the existing `scoutNeed` evidence. That proves Coach inserted Scout as a first
stage. MANUAL Scout and explicit `PLAYER: Scout` routes do not create automatic
continuation authority.

`analyzeScoutContinuationAuthority()` requires an explicit follow-on
deliverable in the original human Play:

- implementation/change work such as implement, fix, patch, update, or build;
  or
- synthesis work such as recommend, propose, design, choose, decide, or produce
  a plan/approach.

Negated mutation language such as `do not modify code` does not fabricate
implementation authority. A reconnaissance-only deliverable such as `tell me
what you find` stops after Scout. Ambiguous intent also stops.

The initial Scout detector now recognizes an explicitly sequenced form such as
`investigate ... and fix it` as reconnaissance followed by action, while still
requiring the existing strong reconnaissance and material-evidence signals.

==================================================
SCOUT OUTCOME AND LOOP SEMANTICS
==================================================

- `COMPLETE` + durable report path + explicit follow-on authority: Coach may
  re-evaluate and dispatch once.
- `COMPLETE` + reconnaissance-only/ambiguous authority: stop after the report.
- `COMPLETE` without a durable evidence path: `UNKNOWN`; do not continue.
- `PARTIAL`: preserve PARTIAL; do not guess that evidence is sufficient.
- `BLOCKED`, `FAILED`, or `UNKNOWN`: preserve that outcome; do not continue.
- no eligible continuation Player: stop; do not recruit, refresh, or retry.

Immediate continuation always filters canonical `scout`. Scout-origin report
markers and Scout-owned context remain the earlier routing guards. Replayed
terminal Scout results are idempotent: once a record leaves `awaiting-scout`, a
duplicate event cannot create a second continuation.
