==================================================
COACH REFRESH OWNERSHIP
==================================================

Coach Refresh owns only:

- deciding that the Combine `refresh-due` selection policy should be used;
- applying absolute V0.1 caps of 5 tryouts and 2 concurrent attempts; and
- providing a callable/CLI activation seam for future trigger policy.

Combine still owns live discovery, ordering, read-only execution, the
existing five-minute attempt timeout, failure classification, game film,
scorecard merging/writes, durable run evidence, and READY-depth-chart
before/after measurement. Formation still owns eligibility and dispatch and
re-reads the same scorecards on every run.

There is no Coach Refresh scorecard, candidate registry, or health store.

==================================================
ACTIVATION SEAM
==================================================

`runCoachRefresh(request)` delegates directly to `runScoutCombine()` with
`selectionPolicy: 'refresh-due'`. The CLI compiles current source and invokes
that callable. This is intentionally trigger-neutral: startup, pre-Scout
routing, scheduled maintenance, and a human Refresh action can later call the
same primitive without moving tryout truth out of Combine.

A no-due run is a successful zero-attempt Combine run. Its normal
`COMBINE-COMPLETE.json` and `COMBINE-RESULT.md` record that refresh was not
needed and that no scorecards changed.

==================================================
DUE-CANDIDATE SEMANTICS
==================================================

Coach Refresh reuses the current Combine scorecard and staleness rules:

- no scorecard: due for a first bounded tryout;
- missing or invalid `lastTryoutAt`: due because the evidence cannot prove
  freshness;
- last tryout older than Combine's existing seven-day staleness window: due,
  regardless of READY/LIMITED/UNKNOWN/callback status;
- last tryout inside seven days: not due, including CALL BACK LATER, UNKNOWN,
  LIMITED, provider-blocked, and READY candidates.

That same seven-day rule is the minimum retry window. It prevents immediate
retries of a failed candidate without inventing a second callback clock or
fake status precision. Within the due set, Combine retains its existing
ordering: new, stale, callback, then recently proven, with candidate id as the
deterministic tie-breaker.

Every discovered candidate now receives a durable consideration containing
candidate id, prior status, last tryout time when known, due/not-due, and the
reason. The Combine completion also records prospects due, prospects actually
selected, applied limits, scorecard conclusions, and READY ids before/after.

==================================================
BOUNDED RETRY BEHAVIOR
==================================================

- Default and absolute Coach Refresh cap: 5 candidates per invocation.
- Default and absolute Coach Refresh concurrency: 2.
- Lower caller-requested limits are honored; higher values are capped.
- Zero, negative, non-integer, or non-numeric limits fail closed.
- Existing per-attempt timeout remains five minutes.
- Recently tried candidates are excluded, not merely ranked last.
- One executor failure is normalized and written independently; sibling
  attempts continue.
- No timer, loop, daemon, automatic retry, or continuous provider polling
  exists in V0.1.

Provider/infrastructure outcomes retain the existing reversible Combine
statuses. No Player is cut. Scorecards for candidates not selected by the run
are untouched.
