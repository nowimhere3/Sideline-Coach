## Architecture Contract Implemented

`projectRoutingEconomics(health, alarmState, preferences, now)` is pure. It:

1. calls the existing `normalizeAlarmFacts` with the same `maxStaleAgeMinutes` preference AlarmEngine uses;
2. adapts the current closed Claude/Codex Stage 2A facts into open string resource-pool/window identities at one internal boundary;
3. reads canonical per-window AlarmEngine states for policy floors without comparing percentages to thresholds;
4. obtains and carries each `ProviderResourcePolicy` from `ResourcePolicyService` without reshaping it;
5. derives remaining fraction, time to reset, pace pressure, base price, policy floor, staleness, observable policy lag, and pool evidence;
6. returns no mutations, cache, polling, persistence, recommendation, or route.

The public contract is:

- `ResourcePoolId = string`
- `RoutingEconomicsSnapshot.pools: Record<ResourcePoolId, RoutingEconomicsPool>`
- each pool carries a string `pool`, evidence, open window list, and optional Stage 2A policy
- each window carries string `id`, data-owned `lengthMin`, optional canonical/derived facts, `price`, optional floor, `stale`, and `policyLag`

Known V1 descriptors are Claude and Codex, each with explicit 300-minute 5-Hour and 10,080-minute Weekly lengths. The formula consumes the descriptor's `lengthMin`; it never derives duration from a window enum or name.

## Economics Semantics

For a proven window:

`pacePressure = (timeToResetMin / lengthMin) / max(remainingFraction, 0.01)`

`price = clamp(pacePressure, 0.05, 10)`

LOW and CRITICAL floors are represented separately as 1.5 and 4.0, sourced from canonical AlarmEngine window state. R1 does not invent a Play duration and therefore does not prematurely bake a duration-dependent floor into base price.

`estimatePoolEconomicCost(pool, burns, expectedDurationMin)` is a pure §11 helper with explicit duration input. It applies a floor only when the canonical scarcity survives the supplied Play duration and splits burn at reset, charging post-reset burn at fresh-window price 1.0. It does not estimate burn or duration.

The S57.1 §11.3 fixture produced:

- Codex A: 5H pace ≈ 0.84, Weekly pace ≈ 3.81, economic cost ≈ 0.215
- Claude B: 5H pace ≈ 0.13, Weekly pace ≈ 0.48, economic cost ≈ 0.100 (within the architecture's ≈ 0.099 rounding)
- B is less than half A despite B showing only 25% remaining on 5H

Codex Weekly's LOW floor is 1.5, below its ≈ 3.81 base price, so it correctly does not lower or replace the higher pace price.

## UNKNOWN And Staleness

UNKNOWN remains first-class:

- absent provider facts produce unknown pool evidence and unknown prices;
- stale facts are identified exclusively by `normalizeAlarmFacts` using canonical `maxStaleAgeMinutes`;
- stale windows omit remaining fraction and reset horizon rather than leaking expired facts;
- a missing reset horizon produces unknown price rather than a guess;
- Stage 2A `evidenceState: 'unknown'` keeps the pool unknown even if another fact is available;
- unsupported seat resource authority maps to `unknown` and creates no fabricated pool/window data.

At exactly 30 minutes old with a 30-minute preference the test fact remains known; at 31 minutes it becomes stale and unknown, matching AlarmEngine's existing greater-than rule.

`policyLag` reports observable evidence/cycle skew without threshold re-evaluation: known-versus-UNKNOWN disagreement, missing-versus-present canonical horizon disagreement, or reset-cycle timestamp disagreement. Threshold-only lag cannot be independently inferred because Stage 2A intentionally persists neither remaining percentage nor evaluation revision; R1 does not guess or violate that boundary. In the live daemon, HealthAuthority's existing synchronous `onChange → AlarmEngine.evaluateTelemetry` chain remains unchanged.
